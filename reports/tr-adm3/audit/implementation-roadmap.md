Prerequisite: land [PR #94](https://github.com/mberatkaya/TerritoryKit/pull/94)'s decoded layer/zoom validation and lookup/render pins through normal review. Independently distribute its immutable rebuilt artifact only in a separately authorized delivery task. Old tile caches remain old until a consumer proves the new selection. This audit deliberately does not duplicate those changes.

Estimates describe implementation effort, not waiting time for municipal access. No v2 rewrite is justified by this sprint. v1.7 already has network-first bounded faces, residual partitioning, organic routing, seed/profile decisions, component-aware islands, barrier/unsupported-edge diagnostics, quality ranking and deterministic IDs. Candidate 631 low-confidence/577 realism-warning districts require classified improvements, not automatic promotion.

Do not add Voronoi, square/hex fallback or H3. Topology validity is insufficient for neighbourhood fidelity. Keep legitimate official source geometry separate from effective gameplay clipping. Each algorithm proposal must carry a failing immutable fixture, declared thresholds, source hashes, migration/ID impact and safe low/unavailable fallback before broader national replay.
