## Confirmed differences and blockers

All **18,856** indexed national MVTs match checksum bytes and decode; all advertise `territory-adm3` but encode `territory_adm3`. Actual zooms are 10:978, 11:3,676, 12:14,202 tiles. All 139,576 tiled feature occurrences match known canonical IDs and geometry-hash metadata; they lack parent/source-version metadata. Hash metadata agreement is not exact rendered coordinate equivalence. #94's separately rebuilt local Fatih tile agrees with its manifest and carries missing fields; only that sample was checked from the fixed output.

Official source preservation is not coordinate immutability: declared original/effective hash changes **1,901**, clipping flags **1,328**, positive internal overlap removals **534**, parent corrections **5**. Parsed geometry serialization differs for **3,339**, and spherical area differs by more than 1 m² for **1,624**; these are different measurements. [Fidelity audit](official-source-fidelity.md).

Canonical ADM0–ADM2 records carry `geoboundaries` provenance and the CLI loads the tracked dataset, while national source-lock attribution describes HDX COD-AB. This is a confirmed provenance/selection mismatch in metadata and code, not evidence that the parent geometries themselves are wrong. A replay must resolve the input-byte lineage before claiming a clean HDX-derived build.

No mobile video file, device logs, selected IDs, MapLibre style/source URL, offline-cache inventory or hosted release receipt was supplied. Production coverage and phone root cause remain **NOT_OBSERVED**. The locked PBF and four municipal snapshots are present and checksum-verified. Three replay barrier bundles verified through the existing reader. The remaining 970 district barrier bundles and ADM0–ADM2 raw archive have not all been independently checked; no national rebuild was started.
